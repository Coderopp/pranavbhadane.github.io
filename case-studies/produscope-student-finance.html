The 'Hisaab' Problem

College students face unique financial challenges that existing apps don't address:

  • Irregular income and spending driven by peer pressure
  • Difficulty tracking expenses across UPI and credit
  • Lack of financial literacy making budgeting harder
  • No student-first financial products in the market

User Personas

👨‍💻 Aarav, 19 — College Topper & OG Coder
Background: Part-time intern, loves cafe hopping
Pain Points: Lacks knowledge about managing income, no structured approach to saving, prioritizes lifestyle over wealth building
Needs: An effective but fun way of incorporating budgeting and saving in day-to-day life
🦋 Meher, 23 — The Social Butterfly
Background: Frequent outings with friends, shopping, spends freely
Pain Points: Wants to learn investing but loses interest, has credit card but doesn't know how to benefit from it
Needs: Trustworthy and interesting way of learning investments, managing budget while out with groups

Solution Features

  • Round-up Savings — Automatic savings from every transaction
  • Emergency Pot — Build safety net with gamification
  • Financial Learning — Gamified lessons and quizzes
  • Spending Analytics — Visual insights into spending patterns
  • Peer Challenges — Social savings competitions
"Solve the 'Hisaab' problem with a student-first approach to money management."

Expected Metrics & Impact

  • 70% adoption of round-up savings feature
  • 5-7 mins/day session time on dashboard and quizzes
  • 60% 30-day retention via gamification
  • <20% churn rate
  • ₹500/user/month in emergency pot savings
  • 15-20% decrease in non-essential spending
  • NPS >70 from post-onboarding surveys

Risks & Mitigations

  • Adoption Risk — Beta pilots, incentives, A/B onboarding, NPS tracking
  • Regulatory — Fintech partners, audits, RBI compliance updates
  • Technical — 20% QA budget, rapid iterations, bug reporting
  • Data Privacy — Encryption, clear policies, security audits

Key Takeaways

  • Student finance requires a fundamentally different product approach
  • Gamification drives engagement and habit formation
  • Financial literacy must be woven into the core experience
  • Peer features create viral growth through word-of-mouth
  • Clear metrics and decision levers enable data-driven pivots
