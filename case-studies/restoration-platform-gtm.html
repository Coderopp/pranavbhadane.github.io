GTM Strategy

Restoration Platform — Product & GTM Strategy

End-to-end product strategy including competitive analysis, user research, and go-to-market planning.

Strategy Overview

This case study documents the comprehensive go-to-market strategy developed for a restoration services platform entering a competitive market.

Market Context

The restoration services industry faces unique challenges:

  • Fragmented service provider landscape with varying quality
  • Customer trust is essential but hard to establish
  • Urgency-driven purchasing decisions
  • Complex stakeholder relationships (homeowners, insurance, contractors)

Strategic Framework

We developed a three-phase GTM approach:

  1. Phase 1: Foundation — Build supply-side network with quality verification
  2. Phase 2: Launch — Targeted geographic rollout with local marketing
  3. Phase 3: Scale — Expand categories and geographies based on learnings

Competitive Analysis

We identified key differentiators against existing players:

  • Transparent pricing vs. opaque quote processes
  • Real-time job tracking vs. phone-based updates
  • Quality guarantees backed by the platform
  • Streamlined insurance claims integration
"In a trust-deficit market, transparency becomes your competitive moat."

Strategic Insights

  • Supply-side quality is the foundation of marketplace trust
  • Geographic focus beats broad, thin coverage in early stages
  • Insurance partnerships can drive significant volume
  • Customer acquisition cost drops significantly with reviews and referrals
